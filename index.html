About

Transavia Roster keeps your roster and notes in this device's storage. It never contacts a server with your data — there is no account, no sync, no analytics.

Online map tiles are off by default; the world outline is bundled with the app so the map works in airplane mode. Turning tiles on requests images from CARTO/OpenStreetMap — that reveals which areas you pan over, not your roster.

Roster times are UTC (Zulu), shown exactly as printed, so block and duty totals are plain arithmetic. Local station times are derived from the airport's time zone. Distances are great-circle.

Not an official Transavia app. It is an independent personal tool, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Transavia. All trademarks belong to their owners.
